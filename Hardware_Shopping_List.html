!
Do not substitute the MOSFET. A standard IRF540N requires 10 V gate drive and will not switch from an ESP32’s 3.3 V output. You specifically need IRLZ44N (note the “L” for logic-level). Acceptable alternatives: IRL540N, IRL3803, AO3400 (SMD).
!
Verify valve type. The solenoid must be Normally Closed (NC) so that the spray is off by default. A Normally Open (NO) valve would spray continuously and only stop when powered — this is unsafe.

Reference

Common Purchasing Mistakes

Review this list before finalising your order.

  • ×
    Buying a basic ESP32 without PSRAM. The TFLite model requires 8 MB PSRAM to load. Confirm the S3 variant with OPI PSRAM before paying.
  • ×
    Buying IRF540N instead of IRLZ44N. The “L” in the part number indicates logic-level gate threshold. IRF540N needs 10 V to switch — ESP32 outputs 3.3 V.
  • ×
    Buying a Normally Open (NO) solenoid. An NO valve sprays by default and stops only when powered. You need a Normally Closed (NC) valve for fail-safe operation.
  • ×
    Skipping the 1N4007 flyback diode. When the solenoid turns off, it produces a voltage spike that can permanently damage the ESP32. This ₹5 diode prevents that.
  • ×
    Using a charge-only USB cable. Charge-only cables lack the data pins needed for programming and serial debug. Test the cable with a file transfer before heading to the field.
  • ×
    Connecting 12 V directly to ESP32. The solenoid circuit is a separate 12 V loop. The ESP32 runs on 5 V USB. They only share a common GND wire.
